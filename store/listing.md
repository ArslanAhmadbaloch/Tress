Shared between the App Store and Google Play. Written to the same rule as
the rest of the app: describe what it records, never what it causes. No
regrowth claims, no before/after promises, no percentages.

## Full description

Hair changes slowly. Too slowly to see in a mirror.

Tress is a private journal for that change. Photograph your hair from the same
five angles, write a line beside each set, and keep track of what you are
actually using — then look back across months instead of trying to remember.

FIVE ANGLES, FRAMED THE SAME WAY
On-screen guides line each shot up with your last one, so what you compare next
month is your hair rather than where you happened to stand.

ANY TWO MONTHS, SIDE BY SIDE
Change spread over months is invisible day to day and obvious across a slider.
Put any two points in your journey next to each other.

A LINE BESIDE EVERY SET
What you changed, how the month went, what you noticed. The part you will not
remember in March.

YOUR STACK, TICKED NOT PLANNED
Whatever you already use — shampoos, topicals, supplements, prescriptions — in
one list, with the days you actually did it. Tress shows how steadily you have
kept to your routine, not how well it worked.

LEARN, WITHOUT BEING SOLD TO
Plain explanations of how hair grows, how studies measure it, and how to read a
claim. Written to explain rather than to advise.

YOUR PHOTOGRAPHS STAY YOURS
There is no account, no sign-in and no server. Your photographs, notes and
routine live in the app's private storage on your own device. They are not
uploaded, not analysed and not shared with anyone. We could not see them if we
wanted to, because they never reach us.

WHAT TRESS IS NOT
Tress is a tracking and documentation tool. It is not a medical device, it does
not diagnose anything, and nothing in it is medical advice. It records change;
it does not cause it. For anything clinical, speak to a qualified healthcare
professional.

TRESS PREMIUM
Premium unlocks photo capture, side-by-side comparisons, your treatment stack
and your full journey history. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial, then a
monthly or yearly subscription at the price shown in the app for your country.
Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled, and you can cancel any time
in your account settings.
